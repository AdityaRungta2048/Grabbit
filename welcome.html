Grabbit is ready 🐰

Turn any website into a spreadsheet in seconds. Free, private, and no account needed.

  1. Pin Grabbit: click the puzzle icon 🧩 in Chrome’s toolbar, then the pin next to Grabbit.
  2. Open it on any page with the icon or Alt+G. Grabbit finds lists and tables automatically.
  3. Not the right data? Click ‹ › to see other lists, or Pick on page and click any item.
  4. Export to Excel, CSV, JSON, or straight into Google Sheets.
