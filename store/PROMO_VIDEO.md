# Grabbit promo video script

For the **Global promo video** field in the Chrome Web Store (a YouTube link).
Length: about 60 seconds. Format: 1920×1080 screen recording with a voiceover, plus big on-screen captions, because many people watch muted.

## Hook (pick one)

| # | Hook | Score | Formula |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A (used below)** | "You are wasting hours copying data from websites. The fix is one click, and it is free. Here is why nobody tells you." | 60 / workable | Contrarian Flip |
| B | "If you still copy product prices into Excel by hand, stop. This page becomes a spreadsheet before you finish reading this." | 59 / workable | The Warning |

Hook A wins because it names the cost (hours of copy-pasting), and "why nobody tells you" opens a question that the video only answers at the end. That keeps people watching through the demo. Its weakest part is specificity: if you time yourself copying one page by hand, put that real time in place of "hours".

## Where to record
Use **books.toscrape.com**, a public sandbox site built for scraping practice. It has a product grid, prices, images and a "next" button, and there's no risk of breaking a real site's terms. For the email beat, use a company site where you're happy to show the contact details, such as your own.

**Runtime:** about 150 spoken words, roughly 60 seconds at 150 words per minute.

## Voiceover text only (to read or paste into a text-to-speech tool)
```
You are wasting hours copying data from websites. The fix is one click, and it is free. Here is why nobody tells you.
Open Grabbit on any page. It finds the list for you: titles, prices, links and images, already in a table.
Wrong list? Click any item, and Grabbit grabs everything that looks like it.
Turn on "Next button", and it scrapes every page while you watch.
Building leads? It finds emails, phones and social profiles, even on the contact page. Or paste a list of sites and grab them all.
Schedule it daily to track prices, and see what's new each time.
So why does nobody tell you? Most scrapers sell credits. Grabbit runs in your own browser, so it's free. No account, no limits, nothing uploaded.
Add Grabbit to Chrome.
```

## Recording checklist
- Use a clean Chrome profile with only Grabbit installed and Grabbit pinned, and zoom the browser to 110–125% so the panel is readable.
- Record each beat as its own clip with OBS (free) or Loom, then cut them together. Speed up the waiting parts (page loads, pagination) 2–4×.
- Use bold captions in brand purple (#7C5CFF) on white, so the video works with the sound off.
- For the YouTube upload, use the title **Grabbit – Free Web Scraper for Chrome (Any Website to Excel in One Click)**, put the store link first in the description, and use `store/promo-marquee.jpg` as the thumbnail.
- Paste the YouTube URL into **Global promo video** in the dashboard.
