# Grabbit Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

Grabbit is a browser extension that extracts data from web pages you choose and exports it to files on your computer.

## What we collect
**Nothing.** Grabbit has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

## How your data is handled
- Grabbit reads a web page's content only when you open Grabbit on that page, or start a multi-page or bulk scrape of pages you listed.
- All processing happens locally in your browser.
- Scraped data stays in the Grabbit panel until you export it. Exports are saved to your own computer, or copied to your clipboard when you choose "Copy" or "Sheets".
- Saved recipes (which elements to scrape on a site) and simple settings live in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. Removing the extension deletes them.
- Grabbit never sells, shares or transfers any data to third parties.

## Permissions
- **activeTab / scripting:** read the page you opened Grabbit on.
- **sidePanel:** show Grabbit's interface beside the page.
- **storage:** keep your recipes and settings on your device.
- **Optional site access:** asked for only when you start multi-page or bulk scraping, to open and read the pages you chose.
